DIBRIS — VIA DODECANESO 35 — NAVIGATION GUIDE
==============================================
(For use with Pepper visitor-guidance demos at the Sgorbissa lab)

BUILDING OVERVIEW
-----------------
DIBRIS is the Department of Informatics, Bioengineering, Robotics and Systems
Engineering of the University of Genoa. The main building at Via Dodecaneso 35
is a multi-storey complex on the slope above the Genoa seafront, in the
Albaro / San Martino area. The Sgorbissa social-robotics lab and the Pepper
robot are located on the ground floor wing.

FROM THE MAIN ENTRANCE
----------------------
Main entrance: from Via Dodecaneso, walk up the short ramp and through the
glass doors. The reception desk is straight ahead.

TO THE SGORBISSA HRI LAB AND PEPPER ROOM
----------------------------------------
1. From the reception desk, walk straight ahead past the lifts on your right.
2. Turn left into the ground-floor corridor.
3. The HRI lab and Pepper experiment room are at the end of this corridor,
   on your right. Look for the door labelled "Laboratorio HRI / Sgorbissa".
4. If a session is running you may see a "Do Not Disturb" sign on the door —
   wait at the bench opposite until the experimenter invites you in.

TO THE DIBRIS SECRETARIAT
-------------------------
1. From the main entrance, turn right at the reception desk.
2. The secretariat is the first door on the right, labelled "Segreteria DIBRIS".
3. The secretariat handles visitor badges, building access cards, and contact
   with the academic staff.

TO PROF. SGORBISSA'S OFFICE
---------------------------
Prof. Sgorbissa's office is on an upper floor of the DIBRIS building. The
exact room number is signposted on the office door directory next to the lift
on each floor. For appointments, please email antonio.sgorbissa@unige.it.

TO THE TOILETS
--------------
Toilets are on the ground floor along the corridor opposite the lifts.
Accessible toilets are next to the main lift. There are additional toilets on
each upper floor near the staircases.

TO THE DIBRIS LECTURE THEATRES
------------------------------
Two large lecture theatres ("Aula Magna" and "Aula 218") are used for DIBRIS
courses and seminars. From the main entrance, take the lift to the relevant
floor — Aula Magna is on the ground floor at the back of the building, and
Aula 218 is on the second floor. Smaller seminar rooms ("Sala riunioni")
are scattered along the upper-floor corridors.

TO THE CAFFETTERIA / COFFEE MACHINES
------------------------------------
There are coffee machines on every floor near the staircases. The nearest
cafeteria-style bar is a short walk along Corso Italia (the seafront road)
or down Via Dodecaneso toward Brignole.

TO THE TRAIN STATION (GENOVA BRIGNOLE)
--------------------------------------
From the DIBRIS main entrance:
1. Walk down Via Dodecaneso toward the seafront.
2. Turn right onto Corso Italia.
3. Continue for roughly 1 km — the station is signposted as "Genova Brignole".
4. Total walking time: approximately 12 minutes.

TO PIAZZA DE FERRARI (CITY CENTRE)
----------------------------------
Take any bus heading west along Corso Italia or Via Casaregis (lines 17 / 31)
and ride to Piazza Cavour or De Ferrari. Walking time is about 25–30 minutes.

EMERGENCY EXITS
---------------
- Main staircase: next to the lifts on each floor.
- Secondary staircase: at the far end of each corridor.
- Evacuation assembly point: the open courtyard on Via Dodecaneso side of the
  building. Follow the green emergency-exit signs.

ACCESSIBILITY
-------------
The main entrance is wheelchair-accessible via the ramp on Via Dodecaneso.
Lifts service all floors. Accessible toilets are on the ground floor.

LANDMARKS NEARBY
----------------
- Genova Brignole train station (~1 km).
- Corso Italia seafront promenade (~250 m downhill).
- San Martino hospital and medical school (across the campus, ~15 min walk).
- Boccadasse fishing village (~25 min walk along Corso Italia).
