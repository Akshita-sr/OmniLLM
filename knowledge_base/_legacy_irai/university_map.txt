BUILDING C — ENGINEERING WING — NAVIGATION GUIDE
=================================================
(For use with IRAI Lab visitor guidance system)

BUILDING OVERVIEW
-----------------
Building C is a 5-storey building (Ground + 4 floors) in the Engineering Wing
of the university campus.

Ground Floor: Reception/Lobby, Security Desk, Seminar Rooms A-100 to A-105,
              Disabled access toilets, Lifts (2), Staircase A and B.
1st Floor: Faculty Offices (B-101 to B-150), Staff toilets, Small kitchen.
2nd Floor: Seminar Rooms (B-201 to B-205), PhD Offices (B-210 to B-230),
           Printing room, Toilets.
3rd Floor: IRAI Lab (Room 305), Server Room (306), User Study Room (307),
           Workshop (308), Breakout/Café (310), Toilets.
4th Floor: Prof. Martinez's office (C-401), Dr. Patel's office (C-402),
           Conference Room (C-410), Archive storage.

DIRECTIONS FROM MAIN ENTRANCE TO KEY LOCATIONS
-----------------------------------------------
To the IRAI Lab (Room 305):
  From the main entrance: Go straight past the security desk.
  Take the lift or Staircase A on your left up to the 3rd floor.
  Turn right out of the lift/staircase. Walk along the corridor.
  Room 305 is on your right, approximately 30 metres from the staircase.
  Look for the "IRAI Lab" sign on the door.

To the Seminar Room B-201:
  From the main entrance: Take the lift to the 2nd floor.
  Turn left out of the lift. Room B-201 is the first room on your right.

To Lecture Hall D-100:
  Lecture Hall D-100 is in Building D (adjacent, connected via covered walkway).
  From Building C main entrance: Exit through the main door.
  Turn right and follow the covered walkway for about 50 metres.
  Enter Building D — Lecture Hall D-100 is immediately on your left.

To Prof. Martinez's Office (C-401):
  Take the lift to the 4th floor. Turn left. Room C-401 is the 3rd door on the left.

To Dr. Patel's Office (C-402):
  Take the lift to the 4th floor. Turn left. Room C-402 is the 4th door on the left.

To the Cafeteria / Main Canteen:
  The main cafeteria is in Building A (Central Building).
  From Building C: Exit via the main entrance. Cross the courtyard (approx. 100 metres).
  Enter Building A through the glass doors. The cafeteria is on the ground floor,
  straight ahead.

To the Toilets (3rd Floor):
  From the IRAI Lab (Room 305): Turn left out of the lab door.
  Walk to the end of the corridor. Toilets are on your right.
  Accessible toilet: Near the lift, same corridor.

To the Workshop (Room 308):
  From the IRAI Lab (Room 305): Turn right out of the lab door.
  Room 308 is two doors down on the right.

To the Breakout/Café Area (Room 310):
  From the IRAI Lab (Room 305): Turn right out of the lab door.
  Walk to the end of the corridor. Room 310 is the last door on the left.

EMERGENCY EXITS
---------------
Staircase A: Near the lifts on each floor (main route).
Staircase B: At the far end of the corridor on each floor (emergency exit only).
Assembly Point: Car park area behind Building C.
