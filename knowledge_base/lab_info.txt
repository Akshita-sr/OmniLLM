ROBOTS AND EQUIPMENT
--------------------
Pepper (SoftBank Robotics): main social-robotics platform. Firmware NAOqi 2.5.
  Used for the OmniLLM Embodied LLM Arena study and earlier cultural-competence
  studies (CARESSES and related projects).
NAO (SoftBank Robotics): smaller humanoid, used for child-interaction and
  educational studies.
TurtleBot platforms: used for the navigation / SLAM courses and for indoor
  autonomous-navigation research.
Workstations: GPU workstations for training and inference, used by PhD students.
Audio / video: lapel microphones, GoPro cameras and a tripod setup for filming
  user studies; the room can be partly screened off to control the visual
  background of the participant.

SAFETY RULES
------------
1. When the Pepper robot is moving, keep a 1-metre safety distance.
2. Do not switch the robot off via the power cable — always shut down via the
   chest button (long press) or "rest" via Choregraphe.
3. Emergency stop: press the orange button on Pepper's back to put motors in
   safety mode; this is the recommended action if the robot starts moving in
   an unexpected direction.
4. Fire exits and the evacuation map are posted next to the door; in case of
   alarm follow the green signs to the staircase on the seafront side.
5. First aid kit is in the secretariat on the ground floor.
6. In an emergency call 112 (Italian general emergency number).

PARTICIPATING IN A STUDY
-------------------------
If you are here as a participant in the OmniLLM Embodied LLM Arena study:
  - You will be asked to sign an informed consent form before the session.
  - The session takes about 30–45 minutes and includes interacting with Pepper
    under several experimental conditions.
  - Voice recordings are transcribed and the original audio is deleted at the
    end of the session.
  - Your data is anonymised. You can withdraw at any time without giving a
    reason and without consequences.
