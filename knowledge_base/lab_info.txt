FACILITIES AND EQUIPMENT
------------------------
Room 305 (Main Lab): 4 robotic platforms (Pepper, NAO, Buddy, TurtleBot4),
  6 workstations with NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs, motion capture system, eye-tracking equipment.
Room 306 (Server Room): 2× NVIDIA DGX A100, NAS storage (200 TB), cooling unit.
Room 307 (User Study Room): Observation room, soundproofed, 8-camera setup, Faraday cage.
Room 308 (Workshop): 3D printers (Ultimaker S5), soldering stations, component storage.
Room 310 (Breakout/Café): Coffee machine, microwave, fridge, whiteboards.

SAFETY RULES
------------
1. Always announce yourself when entering the lab — robots may be in operation.
2. Keep a 1-metre safety perimeter around any moving robot.
3. Do not interact with a robot unless a lab member is present.
4. Emergency stop buttons are located at the entrance (Room 305) and server room.
5. Fire exits: Left corridor from Room 305, descend staircase B.
6. First aid kit: Room 310 (breakout area), near the coffee machine.
7. In an emergency, dial the internal number 999 or call +44 20 7946 0999.
